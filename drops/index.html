COLLECTIONS / RELEASES

Ideas with a little weight.

Drops are the collection side of UNPLUG LABS: releases and experiments designed to give an idea a form beyond another endless feed. Each release should be useful, thoughtful, or worth keeping.

What belongs in a Drop?

A Drop is a focused release rather than an endless content stream. It can gather prompts, objects, interactive experiences, or editorial material around one idea. The format should serve the idea; it should not become a reason to publish for the sake of publishing.

FORMAT 01

Objects

Small, tangible or digital pieces that make an idea easier to carry into everyday life.

FORMAT 02

Experiences

Interactive formats designed to help you choose or try something, rather than only read about it.

FORMAT 03

Field notes

Editorial work that develops a thought with context, clarity, and a point of view.

FORMAT 04

Experiments

Small invitations to test an offline habit or idea in your own way, without scoring yourself.

A release should stand on its own

Every Drop should make its purpose clear, explain what it includes, and work as a useful destination rather than a thin doorway to somewhere else. New releases should be listed here only when their content and availability are verified.
