TOOL 02 / PICK WHAT FITS

An activity menu for real life.

Not every moment has the same amount of time or energy. Browse a few offline ideas by the kind of space you have today, then choose one without turning it into another obligation.

THE DOPAMINE MENU

Choose a category below for one random idea. The choices are examples, not medical advice or a guarantee of a particular mood effect.

Your activity idea will appear here.

APPETIZERS / 5–10 MINUTES

A small change of scene

  • Step outside for fresh air.
  • Make tea or a cold drink.
  • Write down one thought on paper.
  • Water a plant.
  • Stretch or move comfortably.
  • Tidy one small surface.
  • Listen to one song without multitasking.
  • Look at the clouds or trees.

ENTRÉES / 30–60 MINUTES

Give something a little time

  • Walk a familiar route in a different direction.
  • Cook a simple meal.
  • Read a chapter of a book.
  • Draw, paint, or make something by hand.
  • Visit a nearby public place.
  • Learn a small practical skill.
  • Call someone for a proper catch-up.
  • Work on a hands-on hobby.

SIDES / PASSIVE OR LOW EFFORT

Let the day breathe

  • Sit near a window for a few minutes.
  • Listen to rain or everyday sounds.
  • Take a slow route home when practical.
  • Watch the light change in a room.
  • Rest without trying to earn it.
  • Keep a physical book within reach.

The menu is allowed to change

Use the categories as a flexible prompt, not a rule. Rest can be enough. A different choice can be better on a different day. No streaks, no guilt.
