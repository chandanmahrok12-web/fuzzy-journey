06 / THE IDEA↗

Why unplug?

Not anti-tech. Pro-life-outside-the-screen. Start with the philosophy.

The screen is a doorway.
It doesn't have to be the destination.

UNPLUG LABS makes digital things that point you back toward something offline: a walk, a conversation, a sketch, a quiet moment, a small adventure.

Start smaller
than a life overhaul.

Pick one action. Make it yours. The point is not to do more. It's to be a little more here.

A 10-MINUTE RESET

Leave your phone in another room.
Open a window. Notice five things.

That is enough of a beginning. Nothing to track, share, or win.
