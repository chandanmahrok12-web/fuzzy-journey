RELATED PROJECT / ANDROID

Your phone, reclaimed.

OFFLINE_OS is a separate launcher project exploring a calmer Android home screen and a more deliberate relationship with your device. It has its own product identity within the wider UNPLUG LABS world.

A different starting screen

The home screen is often the first thing a person sees when they reach for a phone. Its layout can invite a deliberate action—or make the next distraction effortless. OFFLINE_OS explores the idea that the interface can be calmer, clearer, and designed around intent.

Product boundaries

OFFLINE_OS is related to UNPLUG LABS, but it is not the parent brand's entire website and it should not be presented as the Reality Kit or Dopamine Menu. Product-specific instructions, compatibility details, permissions, release notes, and download links must be verified against the actual app build before being published.

What to verify before installing

  • Confirm the exact package and build source.
  • Review Android compatibility and required permissions.
  • Read current release notes and known limitations.
  • Download only from a verified project release page.
