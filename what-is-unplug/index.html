THE IDEA

Not anti-tech. Pro real life.

UNPLUG LABS is an offline-first digital brand built around one simple belief: technology should help you return to your life, not keep you from it.

What does “unplug” mean here?

It does not mean rejecting every screen or pretending technology has no place in everyday life. It means choosing when technology is useful—and making it easier to close the loop when it is not.

The internet can teach, connect, entertain, and create. It can also turn every spare moment into another scroll. UNPLUG LABS exists to make the next intentional choice feel accessible.

STOP OPTIMIZING. START LIVING.

Not everything that matters can be measured. A walk does not need a streak. A hobby does not need a dashboard. Rest does not need to be productive to count.

The work is to make useful digital experiences that support a bigger offline life: small prompts, practical tools, thoughtful collections, and clear ideas you can take with you.

How the parts fit together

  • Reality Kit: an invitation to choose one manageable offline action.
  • Dopamine Menu: a way to browse activities by the time and energy you have.
  • Drops: collections and experiments that give ideas a form.
  • Field Manuscript: the deeper thinking and notes behind the approach.
  • OFFLINE_OS: a related but distinct launcher project focused on an intentional phone home screen.

No streaks. No guilt.

These are design principles, not a challenge to become a perfectly optimized offline person. You can come back whenever a tool helps. You do not owe it consistency.

Start with a tiny action. Then go do something beyond this page.
